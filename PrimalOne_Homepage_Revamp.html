01 · Timing

Why morning and evening are different

Morning is for covering daily essentials alongside food. Evening is for compounds that support the wind-down of the day. Origin's omega-3s and vitamins D3 and K2 are fat-soluble, which is why Origin is taken with or after breakfast and not on its own.

02 · Magnesium total

Why magnesium lives in the evening

Magnesium is kept in the evening product so the daily total stays within typical supplemental guidance. That's also why Restore shouldn't be combined with other high-magnesium supplements on the same day.

03 · The method

Assess → Sequence → Adapt

Assess how you eat and how you recover. Sequence Origin in the morning and Restore at night if desired. Adapt based on how the routine feels over weeks, not days.
