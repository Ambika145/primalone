## Science & Education Page

## Hero

Headline: Timing over quantity.

Subhead: Why sequence matters more than stacking more ingredients.

## Module 1: The Cabinet Problem

When multiple supplements are taken together, absorption can become competitive. Minerals can interfere with each other. Botanicals can overlap. The result is uncertainty. Sequencing reduces that uncertainty by separating morning essentials from evening recovery.


## Module 2: Why Morning

Breakfast provides a food matrix that supports absorption of fat-soluble nutrients and omega-3s. Origin is designed to sit with that meal, not replace it. One scoop after breakfast is enough when the meal is already there.

## Module 3: Why Evening

Certain compounds are commonly used in evening routines for relaxation and recovery support. Restore places those compounds in the evening window so they do not compete with the morning floor. The two products are designed for different times of day.

## Module 4: Magnesium Total

Restore is formulated so that the evening magnesium dose stays within common supplemental guidance when Origin is the only other product used. This is one reason the two products are sequenced rather than stacked. Do not add other high-magnesium supplements on the same day.
